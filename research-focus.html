Research Focus

Understanding Himalayan glacier systems through integrated remote sensing, UAV observations, and field validation

Research Focus

My research examines glacier dynamics across the Himalaya using a multi-sensor approach. By combining satellite imagery, UAV surveys, and process-based modelling, I investigate how debris cover, surface temperature, and climate variability shape glacier melt.

  • • Glacier mass balance estimation using UAV technology and remote sensing
  • • Development of frameworks for assessing debris cover thickness on glaciers
  • • Field validation techniques for remote sensing data in challenging terrain

These investigations provide decision-makers with improved understanding of cryospheric change, guiding adaptation efforts for downstream communities dependent on glacier-fed hydrological systems.
