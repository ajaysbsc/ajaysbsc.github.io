UAV-Based Glacier Mass Balance Study

Active

Utilizing UAV technology for high-resolution glacier mass balance analysis and debris thickness studies at key Himalayan glaciers. The project integrates RTK-supported photogrammetry with thermal observations to track seasonal variations.
