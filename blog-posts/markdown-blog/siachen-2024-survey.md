# Siachen Glacier Survey 2024

The 2024 field season pushed our UAV workflows higher up the Siachen glacier, capturing accumulation zones that were previously out of reach.

## Field Team
- Ajay Godara
- Dr. Neha Kulkarni
- Lt. Manish Verma (Logistics)

## Daily Highlights
- Sunrise flights between 06:00-08:30 captured pristine snow conditions before katabatic winds increased.
- Thermal camera passes identified previously unmapped meltwater channels entering the glacier body.
- Rapid RTK corrections from the forward base station reduced ground control needs to just four checkpoints.

## Data Products
1. 4 cm orthomosaic spanning 6.2 sq km
2. DEM differencing stack (2022-2024) prepared for sea-level model ingestion
3. 10 m contour planning map delivered to the Siachen base logistics team

> "This is the cleanest dataset we have captured so far; every control point snapped right into place." — Dr. Kulkarni

## Next Steps
We will run the interferometric pipeline next week and publish a summary for the operations directorate. Follow-up sorties are scheduled for late May to validate the meltwater routing model.
