# Ajay Godara

Personal website showcasing glaciology research, field expeditions, and photography from Antarctica, the Himalayas, and beyond.

**🌐 Visit:** [ajaygodara.github.io](https://ajaygodara.github.io/ajaygodara.github.io/)

## About

Glaciologist and researcher at IIT Bombay, exploring ice dynamics through UAV surveys, remote sensing, and field expeditions. This website documents research activities, expedition stories, and captures the stunning landscapes of Earth's glacial regions.

## What's Here

- **🏔️ Research** - Glaciology research, publications, and ongoing projects
- **📝 Blog** - Field expedition stories and research updates
- **📷 Gallery** - Photography from Antarctica, Himalayas, and field surveys
- **🎓 Education** - Academic background and journey
- **🗺️ Field Surveys** - Documentation of scientific expeditions

## Featured Expeditions

- **Antarctica 44th Expedition (2024-25)** - UAV-based glacier mass balance studies at Maitri Station, East Antarctica
- **Himalayan Glacier Surveys** - Field research on glacier dynamics and climate change impacts
- **Remote Sensing Projects** - Satellite-based glacier monitoring and analysis
